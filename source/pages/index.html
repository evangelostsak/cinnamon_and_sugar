Cinnamon & Sugar is a small Greek bakery and coffee shop on Lychener Straße in Prenzlauer Berg. Brioche sandwiches toasted to order, bagels built while you wait, trays of bougatsa still warm from the oven — and coffee that takes breakfast seriously.

Breakfast & brunch

Three good reasons to walk in

Everything on the counter is made for the morning — and most of it is gone by the afternoon.

🥐

Baked every morning

Bougatsa with feta or vanilla cream, spinach-feta pita, sesame rings, croissants with pistachio or nougat. Out of the oven, onto the tray.

🥯

Sandwiches to order

Five bagels and two toasted brioche sandwiches — salmon and egg, bacon and avocado, mozzarella-pesto, mediterranean feta.

☕

Greek coffee culture

Freddo espresso and freddo cappuccino shaken cold, the classic frappé, plus chai, matcha and hot chocolate with cream.
