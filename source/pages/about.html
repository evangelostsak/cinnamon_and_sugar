Our story

A Greek bakery with a Berlin address

Cinnamon & Sugar started with a simple idea: the pastries people grow up with in Greece — bougatsa, kataifi, koulouri, karidopita — deserve a counter in Prenzlauer Berg, next to a coffee worth sitting down for. It is still a small family business, run day to day by the people who bake.

How it works

Early mornings, short menus, nothing left over

The ovens go on before the street wakes up. Filo is layered for the bougatsa, the sesame rings go in, the croissants are proofed and baked so the first ones land on the counter as we unlock the door.

We keep the menu deliberately small — around fifty things, and most of them are made or finished the same day. Bagels and brioche sandwiches are built to order and toasted while you wait, which is why they take a few minutes longer than a pastry from the tray. It's worth it.

By late afternoon most of the baking is gone. That's how we'd like it: we would rather sell out than sell yesterday.

On the counter

What we're known for

🥧

Bougatsa, three ways

Feta, minced meat, or vanilla cream, wrapped in filo and baked in trays. The feta and the vanilla are the two our guests order most.

🍰

A proper dessert case

Ekmek kadayifi, kataifi, karidopita, trigonaki, cheesecake and a Lotus cheesecake — the Greek classics alongside a few Berlin ones.

🧊

Freddo, done right

Freddo espresso and freddo cappuccino, shaken cold with a proper foam, plus the instant-coffee frappé you can't get everywhere.

🥗

Breakfast that holds up

Salmon and egg on toasted brioche, bacon and avocado, Greek yoghurt with honey and granola, or a low-sugar banana bread.

🌱

Vegetarian by default

Most of the bagels, pitas and pastries are meat-free — mediterranean feta, mozzarella-pesto, spinach-feta pita, gourmet with olives.

🛵

We bring it to you

Delivery on Wolt and Uber Eats inside roughly 3 km of the shop, or order ahead and pick it up on your way past.
